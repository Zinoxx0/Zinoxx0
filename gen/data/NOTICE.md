# What's in this folder

`make_data.py` writes everything here. The daily build only reads it.

- `glyphs.json`: outlines of [Inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter), Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors, and
  [JetBrains Mono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono), Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors.
  Both are under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org).
- `icons.json`: tech icons from [skill-icons](https://github.com/tandpfun/skill-icons) (MIT), Instagram and Steam from
  [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) (CC0), and the 42 mark. The logos belong to their owners.
- `palettes.json`: the hero at each moment of the day, through [Atom Plasma](https://github.com/Zinoxx0/Atom-Plasma)'s
  palette engine.
- `libt-run.txt`: a real run of [Libft_Tester](https://github.com/Zinoxx0/Libft_Tester) on my libft.
